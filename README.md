# 🩺 Nephro Healthcare Tracker (Pers Edition) - Version 2.0

Welcome to the Distribution Hub for the **Nephro Healthcare Tracker Ver 2.0**. 

This Open-Source Healthcare Management Toolkit is designed to help in recording and monitoring the Post Kidney Transplant Vitals of Patients. It provides a Structured, Automated home vitals monitoring template and a Tracking Dashboard Ecosystem, designed to help Kidney Transplant Recipients, their families, and caregivers to systematically log the Patient data to optimize from the Follow-up Consultations with their treating Nephrologists.

## 🛠️ The 5-Step Core Patient Tracking Protocol

1. **👤 Patient Info Data Entry:** Fill out your baseline personal background and transplant date metrics once in the yellow cells to set background calculations.
2. **📅 Daily Vitals Log:** Dedicate 2 minutes at the same time every day to track your Blood Pressure, resting Pulse, finger SpO₂ percentage, morning Weight, and total Liquid Intake.
3. **🧪 Serial Lab Results Updates:** Input new lab report metrics chronologically from left to right (oldest to newest) without skipping any columns to keep trend curves accurate.
4. **💊 Medication History Log:** Record every new prescription addition or dosage drop immediately via color-coded drop-down structural labels (Active, Changed, Stopped).
5. **🩺 Export the Doctor Pack:** Prior to each follow-up medical visit, group the **Doctor Referral Report**, **Dashboard**, and **Daily Vitals Log** tabs together and print or export them as a unified 6-page PDF to email to your clinic.
